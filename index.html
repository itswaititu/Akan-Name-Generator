About the Generator

The Akan Name Generator is a simple web application that helps users discover their traditional Akan name based on their date of birth and gender. In Akan culture, names are deeply connected to the day of the week a person is born, reflecting cultural identity and personal characteristics. This project takes a user’s birthdate, calculates the corresponding day of the week, and then displays their Akan name along with its cultural significance. It’s an engaging way to learn about Ghanaian naming system.
